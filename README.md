# Code_Canvas
The "Global Food & Agriculture Statistics" dataset from the United Nations provides extensive data on worldwide food production and agricultural activities, covering multiple countries and regions over several decades. It includes information on various agricultural categories such as crop production, livestock, fisheries, and forestry. The dataset tracks key indicators like production quantities, area harvested, yield per hectare, food consumption, exports, imports, and environmental factors such as land use and irrigation. This rich dataset enables analyses of trends over time, comparisons between countries or income groups, assessments of climate or policy impacts, and studies on food security and nutrition outcomes. It also supports predictive modeling and the creation of interactive visual dashboards to visualize geographic and temporal agricultural patterns, helping to inform decision-making in food and agriculture sectors.
